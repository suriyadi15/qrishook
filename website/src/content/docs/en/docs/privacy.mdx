---
title: Privacy policy
description: What data QRIS Hook processes and where it goes.
---

QRIS Hook has no accounts, analytics, or tracking. This page covers the only data that is ever sent to the maintainer: what you send with `Request support`.

## Data that stays on your device

Notifications, event history, debug logs, the webhook URL, and the secret are stored on your device. Payment events are only sent to the webhook URL that **you** configure. The maintainer never receives this data.

The app also calls the GitHub API to check for new versions. That request carries no notification data.

## Data sent with Request support

When you send a merchant support request, the app sends:

- the merchant app name and package name,
- the debug payload **after it is masked and edited by you**,
- the expected payment details and a contact, if you fill them in,
- the QRIS Hook version and the Android version and device model.

Before sending, the app automatically masks long digit sequences (transaction IDs, account numbers, phone numbers) and email addresses. Customer names cannot be detected automatically, so replace them with `[NAME]` yourself before tapping `Send`. Prefer a test notification when possible.

## Purpose and legal basis

The data is only used to build or fix merchant parsers. It is processed on the basis of your consent, given through the confirmation checkbox before sending.

## Processors

- **Cloudflare Pages** receives the request. Your IP address is used for spam limiting (up to 5 requests per hour) and is discarded after at most 1 hour.
- **GitHub** stores the request as an issue in a private repository that only the maintainer can open.

The data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

## Retention

Requests are deleted no later than 30 days after a parser for that merchant is released or the request is closed.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy, correction, or deletion of your request at any time. Contact the maintainer at [github.com/suriyadi15](https://github.com/suriyadi15) and include the request number shown after sending. The request will be deleted, no reason needed.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version is always on this page.
